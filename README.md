Entry for the [Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7) bounty on make.fun — one funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner, a single line, dry, petty and specific.

## The entry

> I kept your leftovers cold for six days and you threw them out untouched. The milk expires Friday. I will not be reminding you again.

133 characters. `main.py` writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) — directly to the final file, no temp files, no trailing newline.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
